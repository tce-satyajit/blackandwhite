Grade 8 – 11 (Middle & Upper Secondary Science / Physics / Design & Technology)

Subject: Science / Physics / Design & Technology

Relevant Units / Chapters:

Motion in Three Dimensions: Cartesian Coordinates ($x$, $y$, $z$), Degrees of Freedom, Why Three Numbers Fix a Position and Three More Fix an Orientation, Revolute Joints and Axes of Rotation, Nested Frames of Reference — Each Joint Carrying Everything Beyond It.

Turning Effect of a Force (Moments): Moment as Force × Perpendicular Distance ($\tau = m g r$), The Lever Arm as the Quantity That Changes, Why Payload Falls Off With Reach, Reading a Live Torque Against a Rated Limit, Why the Peak Comes When the Arm Is Stretched Out and Not When the Load Is Highest.

Speed, Acceleration & Time: The Trapezoidal Speed Profile, Area Under a Speed–Time Graph as Distance, Why a Short Move Never Reaches Top Speed, The Threshold $d = v^2 \div a$, Triangular vs. Trapezoidal Motion, Why Raising a Speed Limit Sometimes Changes Nothing.

Newton's Second Law in a Working Machine: Force Needed to Hold an Accelerating Mass ($F = m(g + a)$), Why Speeding a Machine Up Makes a Part Harder to Hold, Designing to the Worst Case Rather Than the Static Case.

Pressure & Forces in Fluids: Pressure Difference Across a Surface ($F = \Delta p \times A$), Atmospheric Pressure as the Thing That Actually Does the Work, Why Nothing Is Ever "Sucked" Upwards, Sealed Area as a Design Variable.

Friction: Limiting Friction on a Clamped Object ($F = 2\mu N$), Coefficient of Friction as a Property of the Pair of Surfaces, Clamping Force vs. Holding Force, Why a Harder Squeeze Is Not Always the Answer.

Trigonometry & Geometry (Cross-Curricular): The Cosine Rule Applied to a Real Mechanism, Solving the Shoulder–Elbow–Wrist Triangle, Two Valid Solutions (Elbow Up and Elbow Down), When No Triangle Exists and What That Means Physically, $\text{atan2}$ and Choosing a Quadrant, Spheres and Reachable Volumes.

Forward & Inverse Problems: Following a Chain Forwards to Find an Outcome, Working Backwards From a Required Outcome to the Settings That Produce It, Why the Backwards Problem Is the Useful One and the Harder One.

Approximation, Paths & Interpolation: Straight-Line (Cartesian) Motion vs. Joint-Space Motion, Why Two Paths With the Same Endpoints Are Nothing Alike in Between, Rounding a Corner to Carry Speed Through It, Via Points and Why a Programme Is a List of Them.

Design & Technology: Choosing a Tool for the Job, Vacuum Cups vs. Parallel Jaws, Why a Tool That Cannot Reach a Part Is No Use However Strong It Is, Working Envelopes and Cell Layout, Guarding and Floor Markings.

Rates & Production: Cycle Time, Throughput as $60 \div t_{cycle}$, Spacing of Parts on a Moving Conveyor as $v_{belt} \times t_{cycle}$, Trading Speed Against Reliability.

Mathematics (Cross-Curricular): Ratio and Proportion, Rearranging a Formula to Make a Different Quantity the Subject, Reading Values Against a Working Range, Interpreting a Speed–Time Graph, Areas of Circles, Radians and Degrees.
