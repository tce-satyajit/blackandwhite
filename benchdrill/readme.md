Grade 8 – 10 (Middle & Upper Secondary Science / Physics / Design & Technology)

Subject: Science / Physics / Design & Technology

Relevant Units / Chapters:

Turning Effect of a Force (Moments): Torque as Force × Radius ($\tau = F \times r$), Why the Same Belt Pull Gives Different Torque on Different Pulleys, The Clamp Bar as a Lever, Reading a Demanded Torque Against an Available One.

Machines, Work & Power: Power as the Product of Turning Effort and Turning Speed ($P = \tau \times \omega$), Why a Machine Trades One for the Other and Cannot Create Either, The Fixed-Power Motor as the Real Constraint, Efficiency of a Drive ($\eta$), Rated Power on a Motor Plate.

Gearing & Ratio (Belt and Pulley Drives): The Ratio Rule for Two Pulleys ($n_1 d_1 = n_2 d_2$), Pitch Diameter as the Circle the Belt Actually Runs On, Stepped Cone Pulleys and Discrete Speed Choices, Reading a Speed Chart, Why Gearing Down Multiplies Torque and Divides Speed by the Same Factor.

Circular Motion & Rotational Kinematics: Revolutions per Minute and Radians per Second, Rim Speed from Angular Speed ($v = \omega r$), Cutting Speed ($V_c = \pi D n$), Why a Wide Tool Must Turn Slowly, Angular vs. Linear Quantities.

Friction: Limiting Friction as the Thing That Holds Work Down ($F = \mu N$), Holding Torque from a Clamp ($\tau = \mu N r$), Coefficient of Friction Between Steel and Cast Iron, Belt Friction and Slip as a Force Limit, Why a Clamp Fails Suddenly Rather Than Gradually.

Forces & Materials: Specific Cutting Force ($k_c$) as Force per Unit Area of Chip, Shearing a Material, Why Different Materials Demand Different Forces, Thrust and Torque in a Cutting Process, Break-out at the Far Face as a Peak Load.

Rates, Speed & Time: Feed Rate in mm/min, Feed per Revolution as Rate ÷ Rotational Speed, Drilling Time as Thickness ÷ Feed Rate, Why Spindle Speed Does Not Appear in the Time, Material Removal Rate.

Compound Reasoning & Limiting Factors: Two Independent Limits Acting on One System, Identifying Which Limit Binds and Where the Changeover Happens, Why a Remedy That Helps One Limit Is Useless Against the Other, Testing a Prediction Against a Measurement.

Proportional Reasoning (Cross-Curricular): Direct and Inverse Proportion, Why Two Quantities That Both Scale as $1/n$ Have a Constant Ratio, Cancelling Factors in a Comparison, Recognising When Changing a Variable Cannot Possibly Help.

Design & Technology: Workshop Machine Anatomy — Column, Head, Quill, Spindle and Chuck, Belt Guards and Why They Are Interlocked, Work-Holding and Why Hand-Holding a Drilled Part Is Dangerous, Selecting a Speed for a Material and a Tool, Reading a Machine's Own Data Plates.

Mathematics (Cross-Curricular): Ratio and Proportion, Rearranging a Formula to Make a Different Quantity the Subject, Circumference of a Circle, Radians and Degrees, Reading Values Against a Working Range, Tangents to Two Circles and the Length of a Belt.
