Grade 9 – 10 (Secondary Science / Physics)

Subject: Science / Physics

Relevant Units / Chapters:

Force & Motion: Newton's Second Law ($F = ma$), Free-Body Diagrams, Weight vs Normal Force.

Momentum & Impulse: Momentum ($p = mv$), Impulse–Momentum Theorem ($J = F\Delta t = \Delta p$), Reducing Peak Force by Extending Impact Time.

Work, Energy & Power: Kinetic Energy ($KE = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2$), Energy Absorbed by a Spring and Dissipated by a Damper.

Elasticity: Hooke's Law ($F = kx$), Spring–Damper (Shock Absorber) Systems, Static Deflection ($x = mg/k$).

Friction: Kinetic Friction on a Surface ($F = \mu N$), Braking Distance.
