Grade 8 – 11 (Middle & Upper Secondary Science / Physics / Design & Technology)

Subject: Science / Physics

Relevant Units / Chapters:

Matter & Its Changes of State: Melting and Solidifying, the Melting Range of a Thermoplastic, Why a Material Must Be Heated Before It Can Be Shaped, Re-melting a Surface to Join Two Pieces of the Same Material.

Thermal Expansion & Contraction: Plastic Shrinks as It Cools, Differential Contraction Across a Cooling Part, Why Corners Lift Off a Cold Bed (Warping), Why ABS Warps More Than PLA.

Heat Transfer: Conduction from a Heated Nozzle and a Heated Bed, Rate of Melting as a Limit on Throughput ($Q_{max}$), Why a Hotend Under-Extrudes When Pushed Too Fast.

Volume & Conservation of Matter: Cross-Sectional Area of a Cylinder ($A = \pi r^2$), Volume Flow Rate ($Q = A \times v$), Conservation of Volume Through a Change of Shape — Filament In Equals Road Out, $v_{filament} = (A_{road} \div A_{filament}) \times v_{print}$.

Speed, Distance & Time: Print Time as Path Length ÷ Speed, Summing Time Over Many Layers, The Speed–Quality Trade-off.

Motion in Three Dimensions: Cartesian Coordinates ($x$, $y$, $z$), Independent Axes of Motion, Decomposing a Curved Path into Two Perpendicular Motions, Relative Motion (the Bed Moves in Y, Not the Nozzle), Discrete vs. Continuous Motion (Z Steps, X and Y Sweep).

Approximation & Resolution: Representing a Smooth Slope by a Staircase of Finite Steps, Step Size as the Limit of Accuracy, Why Halving the Step Doubles the Count and the Time.

Additive vs. Subtractive Manufacturing: Building by Adding Material Rather Than Removing It, Slicing a Solid into Plane Layers, Infill as a Strength-vs-Material Trade-off.

Mathematics (Cross-Curricular): Circumference and Area of a Circle, Direct and Inverse Proportion, Ratio of Areas, Rearranging a Formula to Make a Different Quantity the Subject, Reading and Comparing Values Against a Working Range, Estimating Totals by Summation.
