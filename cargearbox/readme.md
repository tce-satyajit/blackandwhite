Grade 8 – 10 (Middle & Lower Secondary Science / Physics)

Subject: Science / Physics

Relevant Units / Chapters:

Force & Motion: Newton's Second Law ($F = ma$), Net Force from a Free-Body Diagram, Balanced vs. Unbalanced Forces.

Turning Effect of Forces: Moment / Torque ($\tau = F \times r$), Turning Effort at a Distance from a Pivot.

Simple Machines: Gears & Gear Trains, Gear Ratio as Input Speed / Output Speed, Mechanical Advantage, the Trade of Speed for Force.

Work, Energy & Power: Energy is Never Created by a Machine — a Gearbox Multiplies Force Only by Dividing Speed; Efficiency ($\eta$) and Frictional Losses.

Friction: Rolling Resistance ($F = C_{rr} m g \cos\theta$).

Forces on an Inclined Plane: Resolving Weight Along a Slope ($F = mg \sin\theta$), Why Hills Demand a Lower Gear.

Mathematics (Cross-Curricular): Ratio & Proportion, Direct and Inverse Variation, Reading Values from a Dial, Interpreting Line Graphs Against Time.
