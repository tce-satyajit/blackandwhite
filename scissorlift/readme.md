Grade 8 – 10 (Middle & Upper Secondary Science / Physics / Design & Technology)

Subject: Science / Physics / Design & Technology

Relevant Units / Chapters:

Pressure in Fluids: Pressure as Force per Unit Area ($p = F/A$), Pascal's Principle and the Transmission of Pressure Through a Liquid, Why a Liquid Cannot Be Compressed and What That Buys You, The Hydraulic Press as a Force Multiplier, Bar and Pascal as Units, Reading a Pressure Gauge Against a Relief Setting.

Turning Effect of a Force (Moments): Moment as Force × Perpendicular Distance ($\tau = F \times d$), Why the Perpendicular Distance Is the Whole Story, The Principle of Moments Applied to a Pinned Linkage, Why a Force Acting Almost Along a Lever Does Almost Nothing.

Machines & Mechanical Advantage: Mechanical Advantage as Load ÷ Effort, A Mechanical Advantage That Changes Through the Stroke, Velocity Ratio and Why It Is the Inverse of the Force Ratio, The Trade Between Force and Distance, Why a Scissor Lift Is Hardest at the Bottom and Fastest There Too.

Work, Energy & Power: Work as Force × Distance ($W = Fs$), Gravitational Potential Energy ($E_p = mgh$), Conservation of Energy Through a Machine, Power as the Rate of Doing Work ($P = W/t$), Hydraulic Power as Pressure × Flow ($P = pQ$), Efficiency as Useful Output ÷ Total Input.

Forces & Equilibrium: Resolving a Force Into Useful and Useless Components, Reaction Forces at a Pin and at a Roller, Why One End of Every Scissor Arm Must Be Free to Slide, Static Equilibrium of a Loaded Frame.

Stability & Centre of Gravity: Centre of Gravity of a Combined Body, Locating the Combined Centre of Gravity by Weighted Average, The Base of Support and the Tipping Line, Overturning Moment Against Restoring Moment, Why Height and Off-Centre Loading Both Make It Worse.

Rates, Flow & Continuity: Volume Flow Rate in Litres per Minute, Why Flow Alone Sets the Speed of a Ram, Volume Swept by a Piston, Why a Fatter Ram Is Slower for the Same Pump.

Circles, Area & Proportion (Cross-Curricular): Area of a Circle from Its Diameter, Why Doubling a Diameter Quadruples an Area, Square Relationships, Direct and Inverse Proportion, Reading a Non-Linear Relationship off a Graph or a Gauge.

Trigonometry (Cross-Curricular): Sine and Cosine of an Angle in a Right-Angled Triangle, The Cosine Rule Applied to a Real Mounting Triangle, Height and Span of a Scissor as Functions of One Angle, Why One Angle Fixes Every Position of the Machine.

Design & Technology: Anatomy of a Hydraulic Power Pack — Motor, Pump, Tank, Valve and Relief, What a Relief Valve Is For and Why It Is Not a Fault When It Opens, Pinned and Rolling Joints, Load Charts and Rated Capacity, Castors as a Transport Feature Rather Than a Working One, Why Lifts Are Chocked Before Anyone Works Under Them.
