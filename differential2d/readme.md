Grade 8 – 11 (Middle & Upper Secondary Science / Physics)

Subject: Science / Physics

Relevant Units / Chapters:

Circular Motion: Arc Length ($s = R\theta$), Angular vs. Linear Speed ($v = \omega r$), Why Points on Different Radii Move at Different Speeds.

Turning Effect of Forces: Torque ($\tau = F \times r$), Torque at the Wheel, Turning Effort Shared Between Two Outputs.

Simple Machines & Gear Trains: Bevel Gears, Epicyclic (Planetary) Action, the Differential Relation $\omega_{left} + \omega_{right} = 2\,\omega_{carrier}$, Averaging Mechanisms.

Force & Motion: Newton's Second Law ($F = ma$), Net Driving Force, Rolling Resistance.

Friction: Limiting Friction and Traction ($\tau_{grip} = \mu N r$), Slipping vs. Rolling, Why the Wheel with Least Grip Sets the Limit.

Work, Energy & Power: A Machine Redistributes Motion Without Creating Energy; What One Output Gains the Other Loses.

Mathematics (Cross-Curricular): Ratio & Proportion, Arithmetic Mean as a Physical Constraint, Rearranging a Linear Relation, Reading Dials and Interpreting Time-Series Graphs.
